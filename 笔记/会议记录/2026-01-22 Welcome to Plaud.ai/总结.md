# 总结


## About Plaud
Plaud is building the world's most trusted **AI work companion for professionals**, designed to elevate productivity and performance through advanced note-taking solutions. Trusted by over 1,500,000 users worldwide, Plaud’s mission is to **amplify human intelligence** by capturing, extracting, and utilizing everything you say, hear, see, and think.

## Plaud Intelligence
From capturing ideas on Plaud devices to retrieving and applying insights across Plaud App, web, and desktop — **Plaud Intelligence powers every experience in the ecosystem.**

### Capture with multimodal input
* **Record or import audio**: Capture ideas on any Plaud device or import recordings into the app.
* **Press or tap to highlight**: Mark key moments in real time.
* **Input text to add context**: Supplement recordings with typed notes.
* **Enrich with images**: Add visual context for a complete record.

### Extract AI transcripts & contextual summaries
* **AI transcription**: Supports 112 languages, with speaker labels and customizable vocabulary.
* **Automatic summaries**: Generate multiple summaries from a single conversation using 10,000+ expert templates.
* **Powered by leading LLMs**: GPT-5.2, Claude Sonnet 4.5, Gemini 3 Pro, and more.

### Utilize intelligence across your workflow
* **Ask Plaud**: Query your notes for insights, generate content, and save as structured notes.
* **AutoFlow**: Automate transcription, summaries, and delivery using predefined rules.
* **Cross-platform sync**: Access files on Plaud App, web, and desktop, with unlimited cloud storage.
* **Export, share, and integrate**: Seamlessly move insights into your workflow for maximum efficiency.

## Our products

**Plaud Note Pro**  
Elevate every interaction with the world's most advanced AI note taker. Built for high-leverage professionals, Plaud Note Pro captures studio-grade audio up to 5 meters away with AI beamforming and smart dual-mode recording. During critical discussions, press to highlight key moments as they happen, keeping decisions and next steps clearly marked.

**Plaud Note**  
Slim and credit-card-sized, Plaud Note stays within reach to record meetings and calls with one long press. Dual MEMS microphones provide clear audio pickup up to 3 meters, ensuring reliable capture for your professional workflow. Transform your conversations into organized notes effortlessly, so you can focus on the ideas that move your work forward.

**Plaud NotePin**  
Capture conversations in motion without breaking your flow. Plaud NotePin offers hands-free recording in a lightweight, wearable design that fits your style. Whether you are in a walk-and-talk or a spontaneous brainstorm, it captures clear audio up to 3 meters. Stay mobile and present while Plaud NotePin transforms your on-the-go moments into organized, actionable progress.

**Plaud NotePin S**  
Stay present while Plaud NotePin S captures the conversations that matter. In meetings or on the move, the tactile record button delivers reliable capture. Long press to record and short press to highlight key moments. With clear audio pickup up to 3 meters, NotePin S captures conversations wherever work happens.
