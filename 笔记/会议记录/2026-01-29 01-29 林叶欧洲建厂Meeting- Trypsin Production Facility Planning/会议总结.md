# 会议总结

## Key Points
---
- The meeting began with a discussion on sourcing raw materials for a new project, with a target production volume of 600 to 800 tons per year. It was established that sufficient sources exist in other European countries without competing with Spanish suppliers.
- A major concern raised was wastewater treatment at the current facility, which produces 30 tons of wastewater and 5 tons of by-products weekly. The primary issue is the odor generated from by-products at 90 degrees Celsius.
- A proposed solution for the odor is to install a thermocombustor in a new facility to burn residual solvents and odors, which would help meet stricter Italian environmental regulations.
- The current wastewater by-products are classified as category three, costing about 50 euros per ton for third-party treatment.
- The discussion shifted to the filter cake residue from the extraction process. The team lacks experience with trypsin production waste, as the relevant line was previously dedicated to pancreatin.
- The filter cake from the proposed new process (using water and ammonium sulfate) was discussed. In China, this is sold for animal feed. In Italy, it could be classified as waste (category 1), for animal feed (category 2), or for biogas (category 3), with varying costs.
- The facility has potable municipal water, and a Certificate of Analysis (COA) can be provided. The site has a 15,000-volt electricity supply (400 watts peak power) and plans to install 500 megawatts of photovoltaic panels for green electricity.
- There are 30,000 square meters of land available for new construction. Current buildings are fully utilized, and adapting an existing production line poses significant cross-contamination and compatibility challenges.
- A new pharma-grade industrial building is roughly estimated at €1,500 per square meter. A past 1,500 sq. meter project was quoted at €5-6 million. The required space for the new production is estimated at 5,000 sq. meters.
- Labor costs in Italy are significantly higher than in China, with a low-level operator costing around €3,500 per month for a 7-hour workday.
- The construction time for a new 1,500 sq. meter facility is estimated at one and a half years, assuming authorizations are in place. The project will target pharma-grade (GMP) production.
- Equipment will be sourced mostly from China, but a specialized 40-square-meter lyophilizer (estimated at €1.2 million) will be purchased in Italy.
- For raw materials (pancreas), Poland was identified as the best source for quality and cost (around €3/kg), while Spain is much more expensive (up to €16/kg).
- It was agreed that the current meeting served as a preliminary discussion, with a more detailed, in-depth meeting scheduled for March 27th.
## Decisions Made
---
- The target production capacity for the new project will be between 600 and 800 tons per year.
- A site visit is scheduled for March 27 for key stakeholders to inspect the current facility.
- A dual approach will be taken to analyze the filter cake by-product: a chemical analysis report will be sent from China, and a physical sample will be sent to Italy for local analysis.
- The new facility will target pharma-grade production, not food-grade.
- A 40-square-meter lyophilizer for the new facility will be purchased in Italy from Don Flon. Other equipment will likely be sourced from China.
