# 会议要点

### Elevating the Customer Relationship
To escape price-driven negotiations, we must shift from a transactional supplier to a strategic partner by engaging multiple stakeholders across the customer's organization.
-   Engage stakeholders beyond procurement, including Operations, R&D, and Legal, who have different priorities and veto power.
-   Reframe conversations away from price by highlighting non-price value like innovation, efficiency, or risk reduction to other departments.
-   Proactively request strategy meetings and present new technical innovations to align on long-term goals and access key decision-makers.
### De-risking the Supply Chain
The customer’s primary concern is supply chain reliability, which is a powerful negotiating lever against purely price-based demands.
-   Supplier switching in regulated industries introduces significant cost, friction, and risk for the customer due to regulatory re-approval.
-   Operational stability is a key priority for a customer's factory, creating internal resistance to changing suppliers.
-   A credible backup production solution is more critical to customers than the geographic location of the primary factory.
### Verifying Customer Demands
Stated customer requirements, especially from procurement, may be negotiating tactics rather than true business or regulatory constraints.
-   Independently verify the true regulatory barriers, as a customer’s stated reason may obscure the actual issue.
-   Triangulate information by engaging other functional leaders to confirm if a directive is a company-wide strategic priority.
-   Recognize that requests to change operations may be a negotiating tactic or a signal of their internal risk management needs.
