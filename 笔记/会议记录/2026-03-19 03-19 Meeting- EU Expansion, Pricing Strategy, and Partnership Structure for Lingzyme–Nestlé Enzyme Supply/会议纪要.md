[19:39-23:30] **Lingzyme's core competitive advantage is identified as its unique, proprietary purification technology for enzymes.**
- Hillhouse views Lingzyme as a potential leading player in niche pharmaceutical and food-grade enzyme markets due to its technology.
- The core technology is a proprietary affinity chromatography resin developed in-house, which is crucial for enzyme purification from both animal sources and fermentation.
- This technology, typically very expensive and used for small-volume, high-value purifications in pharma (e.g., antibodies), has been adapted by Lingzyme for mass production of enzymes.
