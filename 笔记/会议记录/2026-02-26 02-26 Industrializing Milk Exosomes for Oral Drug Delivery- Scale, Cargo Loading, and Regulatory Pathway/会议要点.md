# 会议要点

### Strategic R&D and Commercialization
The team outlined a bifurcated strategy for funding, scaling, and managing new biotech innovations.
- Fund high-risk research with grants and finance proven discoveries through industry partnerships.
- Partner with universities on IP by taking a majority stake to fund patent maintenance.
- Scale production from lab to industrial quantities is a critical bottleneck for commercialization.
### Advanced Drug Delivery and Production
Exosomes present a breakthrough opportunity for drug delivery, but introduce new production challenges.
- Exosomes offer a non-viral, natural alternative for efficient drug delivery into cells.
- Using naturally sourced exosomes solves scalability but requires new methods for loading therapeutics.
- Assign dedicated production leads at each site to free up leadership for critical problem-solving.
